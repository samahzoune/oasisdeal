Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: July 2026

We believe in being completely upfront about how OasisDeal (oasisdeal.com) makes money. This disclosure explains our affiliate relationships in plain language, so you know exactly how a free service pays its way — and why it never costs you a penny more.

1. How OasisDeal makes money

OasisDeal is free to use, and we don't charge you any fees. Many of the outbound links on our site — including price comparisons, "book", "see plans", "check price" and "claim" buttons — are affiliate links. When you click one and then book, buy or file a claim with the partner, that partner may pay us a commission or referral fee. Your price never changes: you pay exactly what you would pay booking directly with them. Those commissions are what keep our search and content free to run.

2. What an affiliate link is

An affiliate link contains a tracking code that tells the partner you arrived from OasisDeal. If you complete a qualifying action (such as a booking or purchase, sometimes within a set time window), the partner attributes it to us and may pay a commission. If you don't complete an action, or you clear your cookies, no commission is earned — but either way, your price is unaffected. Commissions are paid by the partner out of their own margin, not added to your bill.

3. Our partners

We may earn commissions from trusted travel partners, including but not limited to:

  • Travelpayouts / Aviasales — flight search and booking
  • Stay22 — hotels and stays
  • Intui.travel — airport transfers
  • AirHelp — flight-delay and cancellation compensation
  • eSIM providers (such as Yesim, Airalo and Saily) — travel data plans
  • Car-rental and other travel partners we may add over time

These partners provide the actual bookings and services. When you click through, you are transacting with them under their own terms and privacy policies — OasisDeal is not the seller (see our Terms & Conditions).

4. Our editorial independence

Commissions never change the prices you see, and they do not bias our comparisons or rankings. Where we rank options, we rank them by criteria that benefit you (such as price, cheapest first) — not by which partner pays us the most. We show live prices and honest options so you can choose what's best for you. If a deal isn't good, we won't pretend it is, and our guides and articles aim to give useful, honest information rather than push a sale.

5. Accuracy of prices and offers

Prices, availability and offers shown on OasisDeal are indicative and are supplied by our partners; they can change at any time, and some are estimates or starting ("from") prices. Always confirm the final price and conditions on the partner's website before you book or pay. We are not responsible for partner prices, availability or the services they provide.

6. No guarantees

We do our best to keep information accurate and partners reputable, but we can't guarantee any particular result, price, saving or compensation outcome. Your experience with a partner is between you and that partner.

7. Why we disclose this

We share this in the spirit of transparency and in line with consumer-protection and advertising rules on affiliate relationships — including the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Endorsement Guides and similar guidance in other regions. You deserve to know how a free service is funded, and we'd rather earn your trust than hide the ball.

8. Contact

Questions about how we make money or our partner relationships? Reach us at our contact page or email hello@oasisdeal.com.
