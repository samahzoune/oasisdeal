Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: July 2026

We believe in being upfront about how we make money. Here it is, in plain language.

How OasisDeal makes money

OasisDeal is free to use. When you book a flight, hotel, car, or airport transfer, buy a travel eSIM, or file a compensation claim through a link on our site, our partner may pay us a commission. Your price never changes — you pay exactly what you'd pay booking directly with them. That commission is what keeps our search free and running.

Our partners

We earn commissions from trusted travel partners, including:

  • Travelpayouts / Aviasales — flights
  • Stay22 — hotels and stays
  • Intui.travel — airport transfers
  • AirHelp — flight-delay compensation
  • eSIM providers — travel data plans

Our independence

Commissions never change the prices you see, and they don't bias our comparisons. We show you the live prices and options from our partners so you can choose what's best for you — not what pays us most. If a deal isn't good, we won't pretend it is.

Why we disclose this

We share this in the spirit of transparency and consumer-protection guidelines (such as the U.S. FTC's rules on affiliate relationships). You deserve to know how a free service pays its way.
