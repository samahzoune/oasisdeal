Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

OasisDeal respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

We keep things minimal. When you search, we process the trip details you enter (like route and dates) to show you results. If you contact us, we receive the name, email, and message you send. We also use basic, privacy-friendly analytics to understand how the site is used.

How we use it

  • To run flight and hotel searches and show you results.
  • To help you check and submit flight compensation claims through our partner.
  • To respond to your messages.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone, and we don't send spam.

Third-party services

We rely on trusted providers to run OasisDeal — for example our hosting provider and our search and affiliate partners (Travelpayouts/Aviasales for flights, Stay22 for hotels, and AirHelp for compensation). These services have their own privacy policies. When you click through to a partner, their privacy policy applies to your activity on their site.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you at any time. Just email us and we'll take care of it.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at our contact page or email hello@oasisdeal.com.
