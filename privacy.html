Privacy Policy

Plain-English version: we don't sell your data, and we keep collection to a minimum.

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Who we are

OasisDeal ("we", "us") operates the website at oasisdeal.com, where we share curated product deals and coupon codes. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter email. If you subscribe, we store the email address you give us so we can send you deals. Nothing more.
  • Messages you send. If you contact us, we keep your message and email to reply.
  • Basic usage data. Like most websites, our host may collect anonymous analytics (pages visited, country, device type) to help us improve the site. This does not identify you personally.

Affiliate links

Many links on OasisDeal are affiliate links. When you click one and make a purchase, the retailer (such as Amazon or AliExpress) may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. To do this, the retailer may place a cookie in your browser to credit us for the referral. We never see your payment details — those stay between you and the store.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies needed for the site to function and for the affiliate referrals described above. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work, but affiliate referrals may not be tracked.

How we use your information

  • To send you the deals newsletter you signed up for.
  • To respond to your messages.
  • To understand which deals are popular and improve the site.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We don't send spam, and every newsletter includes a one-click unsubscribe link.

Third-party services

We rely on trusted providers to run OasisDeal — for example our hosting provider and affiliate networks. These services have their own privacy policies. When you click through to a retailer, their privacy policy applies to your activity on their site.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you at any time. Just email us and we'll take care of it.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at our contact page or email hello@oasisdeal.com.
