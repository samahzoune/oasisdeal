Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

OasisDeal ("OasisDeal", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect when you use oasisdeal.com and our related pages (the "site"), how and why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights and choices you have. Please read it carefully. By using the site, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

1. Who we are

OasisDeal operates this site as an independent travel price-comparison and information service. For the purposes of data-protection law, OasisDeal is the "controller" of the personal data processed through the site. If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, you can contact us at hello@oasisdeal.com or through our contact page.

2. The information we collect

We deliberately keep data collection to a minimum. We do not require you to create an account to use the site. The information we process falls into three categories:

a. Information you give us

  • Contact details: when you use our contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you choose to send us.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: if you subscribe to our newsletter, we receive and store the email address you provide.
  • Search inputs: when you use a search tool, we process the trip details you enter (such as origin, destination, dates and the country you're travelling to) purely to display relevant results and prices.

b. Information we collect automatically

  • Usage and log data: like most websites, our hosting and content-delivery provider automatically records technical information such as your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring pages, the pages you view and the date and time of your visit.
  • Approximate location: we may derive an approximate location (such as your nearest city or country) from your IP address so we can show you relevant local prices and departure points. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Analytics data: we use privacy-friendly, aggregated analytics to understand how the site is used and to improve it. This data is aggregated and is not used to personally identify you.

c. Cookies and similar technologies

We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage) to run the site, remember your preferences (like language and cookie consent) and measure usage. You control non-essential cookies through our consent banner. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

3. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate the site and run flight, hotel, car, transfer and eSIM searches and show you results and live prices;
  • Help you check eligibility for, and submit, flight-delay compensation claims through our partner;
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support;
  • Send you our newsletter and travel updates, where you have asked to receive them;
  • Measure and analyse how the site is used so we can maintain, secure and improve it;
  • Detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse, security issues and technical problems;
  • Comply with our legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where data-protection law (such as the EU/UK GDPR) applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for non-essential cookies, analytics and marketing emails. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure and improve the site and understand how it is used, in a way that does not override your rights.
  • Performance of a request — to respond to a message you send us or provide a feature you ask for.
  • Legal obligation — where we must process data to comply with the law.

5. Marketing and our newsletter

We only send you marketing or newsletter emails if you have signed up to receive them. Every newsletter includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can opt out at any time by using that link or by emailing us. We do not send spam and we do not sell your email address.

6. How we share your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. We share data only with the trusted service providers and partners we need to run OasisDeal, and only for the purposes described here:

  • Hosting & infrastructure — our website and content are hosted and delivered through a cloud infrastructure provider.
  • Email delivery — we use an email service provider to deliver contact-form messages and newsletter emails.
  • Analytics — a privacy-friendly analytics provider helps us measure aggregate usage.
  • Search & affiliate partners — including Travelpayouts / Aviasales (flights), Stay22 (hotels), Intui.travel (airport transfers), AirHelp (compensation), our eSIM partners, and Trustpilot (reviews).

Each of these partners is an independent controller of the data it collects and has its own privacy policy. When you click through to a partner's website or interact with a partner widget, that partner's privacy policy applies to your activity with them. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, court order or a valid request from a public authority, or to protect our rights, users or the security of the site.

7. International data transfers

Our partners and service providers may process data in countries outside your own, including outside the European Economic Area. Where personal data is transferred internationally, our providers rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms) to protect it in line with applicable law.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Contact-form messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and our records of it; newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe; and technical log and analytics data is kept for a limited period in aggregated or short-lived form. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

9. How we protect your data

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including serving the entire site over encrypted HTTPS connections and limiting who can access data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access — ask what personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data ("the right to be forgotten");
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data;
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format;
  • Objection — object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on your consent, withdraw it at any time;
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email hello@oasisdeal.com and we'll take care of it, usually within a reasonable time and in any case within the period required by law.

11. Children's privacy

The site is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 16 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers offer "Do Not Track" (DNT) or Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals. Because there is no common industry standard for interpreting these signals, we handle privacy through our cookie-consent banner rather than responding to DNT signals directly. You can always decline non-essential cookies via the banner.

13. Third-party links

The site contains links to third-party websites and services that we do not operate or control. This policy does not apply to those sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you visit through our links.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology or the law. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes may be highlighted on the site. Your continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

15. Contact us

If you have any questions, concerns or requests about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please reach us through our contact page or email hello@oasisdeal.com. We read every message.
