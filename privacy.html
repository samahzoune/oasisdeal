Privacy Policy

Plain-English version: we don't sell your data, and we keep collection to a minimum.

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Who we are

OasisDeal ("we", "us") operates the website at oasisdeal.com, a flight price-comparison site that helps you find and compare cheap flights. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Search details. When you search for a flight, your route, dates and passenger count are sent to our flight-search partner to return results. We don't need your name or login to search.
  • Messages you send. If you contact us, we keep your message and email to reply.
  • Basic usage data. Like most websites, our host and analytics may collect anonymous data (pages visited, country, device type) to help us improve the site. This does not identify you personally.

Affiliate links

When you search and book a flight through OasisDeal, you're sent to an airline or travel agency (via our partner Travelpayouts/Aviasales) to complete the booking. If you book, that partner may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you, and may place a cookie in your browser to credit the referral. We never see or store your payment details — those stay between you and the airline or agency.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies needed for the site to function and for the booking referrals described above. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work, but referrals may not be tracked.

How we use your information

  • To run flight searches and show you results.
  • To respond to your messages.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone, and we don't send spam.

Third-party services

We rely on trusted providers to run OasisDeal — for example our hosting provider and our flight-search and affiliate partner (Travelpayouts/Aviasales). These services have their own privacy policies. When you click through to an airline or travel agency, their privacy policy applies to your activity on their site.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you at any time. Just email us and we'll take care of it.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at our contact page or email hello@oasisdeal.com.
