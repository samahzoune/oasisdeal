Madrid Travel Guide — Best Time, Itinerary & Budget

Published 2026-07-07 · By the OasisDeal team

Madrid is grand plazas, world-class art, buzzing tapas bars and a late-night energy all its own. Here’s the full plan: when to go, a 3-day itinerary, what it really costs, and how to save.

Best time to visit

  • April–June & September–October: warm, sunny and the sweet spot.
  • July–August: very hot — locals escape the city.
  • Winter: mild, quiet and cheapest.
  • Madrid eats and goes out late — plan a siesta and a late dinner.

The perfect 3-day itinerary

  • Day 1 — the Art Walk: the Prado, the Reina Sofía (Picasso’s Guernica) and a stroll in Retiro Park.
  • Day 2 — old Madrid: Plaza Mayor, the Royal Palace, Mercado de San Miguel and a tapas crawl.
  • Day 3 — day-trip: medieval Toledo or the fairy-tale aqueduct and castle of Segovia.

What it costs: a real budget

  • Hotels, 3 nights: $240–450.
  • Food: $110–200 (tapas and menú del día are great value).
  • Attractions (Prado, Royal Palace): $60–120.
  • Transport (metro): $20–40.
  • On-the-ground total: ~$430–810 per person.
  • Flights from the US: $450–850 round-trip.

How to save

  • The Prado and Reina Sofía have free evening hours — go then.
  • Retiro Park and the grand plazas are free to wander.
  • Fill up at lunch on the menú del día, and graze on cheap tapas.
  • The metro is cheap and the centre is very walkable.

Don’t miss

  • The Prado — one of the world’s great art museums.
  • Retiro Park and its glass palace.
  • Plaza Mayor and a tapas crawl.
  • A flamenco show at night.

Getting there & around

  • Fly into Madrid–Barajas (MAD); the metro reaches the centre in ~30 minutes.
  • The metro is cheap and extensive, and the centre is very walkable.
