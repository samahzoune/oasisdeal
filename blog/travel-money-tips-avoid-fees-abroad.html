Travel Money Tips: Avoid Fees Abroad

Published 2026-07-01 · By the OasisDeal team

Exchange rates and card fees quietly eat into your travel budget. A few smart choices keep more money in your pocket.

Keep more of your money

  1. Always pay in the local currency, not your home currency — “dynamic currency conversion” hides a bad rate.
  2. Use a card with no foreign-transaction fees, and tell your bank you’re travelling so it isn’t blocked.
  3. Withdraw larger amounts less often to cut per-withdrawal ATM fees, and decline the machine’s conversion.
  4. Carry a little local cash for taxis, tips and small shops that don’t take cards.
  5. Avoid airport exchange counters — their rates are usually the worst you’ll find.
