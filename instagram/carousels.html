How to use: each carousel below = one Instagram post. Click ⬇ Download PNG above every slide to save it at full resolution (1080×1350). Then on Instagram: + → Post → select all 5 slides in order → paste the caption → publish. Captions & hashtags are at the bottom of each carousel.

Tip: if a cover photo exports blank when you open this file locally, open it on your live site instead (oasisdeal.com/instagram/carousels.html) and download there.
