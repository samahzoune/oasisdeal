Compensation FAQs

Which flights are covered?+

Flights departing from an EU airport on any airline, and flights arriving in the EU on an EU airline, are generally covered — that includes many routes to and from Morocco. Your eligibility check confirms it instantly.

What counts as a valid claim?+

Arriving 3 or more hours late, a cancellation with short notice, or being denied boarding due to overbooking — as long as the airline was at fault (not extraordinary events like severe weather).

How much does it cost me?+

Nothing to check, and claims are no-win, no-fee. If the claim succeeds, a success commission is taken from the compensation and the rest is yours. If it doesn't win, you pay nothing.

How far back can I claim?+

Usually flights from the last 1–3 years, and longer in some countries. If you've had a bad flight recently, it's worth checking.
