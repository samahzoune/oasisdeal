Good to know

eSIM FAQs

What is a travel eSIM?+

An eSIM is a digital SIM you install by scanning a QR code — no plastic card. It gives you a local data plan abroad, so you skip expensive roaming and stay online from the moment you land.

Will it work on my phone?+

Most phones from the last few years support eSIM — iPhone XS and newer, Google Pixel 3 and newer, and recent Samsung Galaxy models. Your phone also needs to be carrier-unlocked. If in doubt, check your settings for an "Add eSIM" option.

Can I keep my normal number?+

Yes. The eSIM handles data only and runs alongside your usual SIM, so you keep your number for calls and texts while using the eSIM for internet.

When should I install it?+

Install it at home before you travel, while you have Wi-Fi. It stays inactive until you arrive, then connects automatically — so you land with working data.
