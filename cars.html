Good to know

Car rental FAQs

Does OasisDeal charge a booking fee?+

No. We compare prices and send you to the rental partner to complete your booking. You pay the same price you'd pay there — we may earn a small commission from the partner at no extra cost to you.

How do you find cheap car rentals?+

Our search checks hundreds of suppliers at the same time for the same location and dates — big names like Hertz, Avis and Europcar alongside trusted local companies — then shows you the lowest total price.

Should I take full insurance?+

Most cars come with a large excess (deductible). Full coverage that brings the excess to zero is usually much cheaper bought through the comparison site than at the rental desk — and it saves arguments at pickup.

What's the minimum age to rent?+

Usually 21, and drivers under 25 may pay a young-driver fee. You'll need a valid licence held for at least a year and a credit card in the main driver's name for the deposit.
