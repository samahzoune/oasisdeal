Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains what cookies are, how OasisDeal uses them, and how you can control them.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device to make it work, remember your preferences, and understand how it's used. Similar technologies (like local storage) work the same way.

2. Cookies we use

  • Essential — needed for the site to function: your language choice and your cookie-consent preference. The site can't work properly without these.
  • Analytics — privacy-friendly measurement to understand how the site is used, so we can improve it. This data is aggregated and not used to identify you.
  • Partner & affiliate — when you interact with a partner widget or click through to a partner, that partner may set cookies to run their tools and to attribute the referral (which is how we earn a commission at no extra cost to you).

3. Third-party services

We use trusted partners whose scripts may set their own cookies, including Travelpayouts / Aviasales (flights), Stay22 (hotels), Intui.travel (airport transfers), AirHelp (compensation) and Trustpilot (reviews). Each has its own cookie and privacy policy that applies to your activity with them.

4. Managing cookies

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's cookies in your browser. You can also block or delete cookies entirely in your browser settings — but note that declining some cookies may limit certain features, such as live price widgets and partner tools.

5. Consent

For non-essential cookies, we ask for your consent via the banner. Essential cookies are always active because they're required for the site to work.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies? See our Privacy Policy, reach us at our contact page, or email hello@oasisdeal.com.
