Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies are, how and why OasisDeal (oasisdeal.com) uses them, which cookies are set when you use the site, and how you can control them. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy. By continuing to use the site, or by accepting cookies through our banner, you consent to our use of cookies as described here.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device (computer, tablet or phone) when you visit. They allow the site to work, to remember your choices and to understand how it is being used. We also use similar technologies, such as browser local storage, which work in much the same way. In this policy, we refer to all of these simply as "cookies".

2. Types of cookies

  • Session cookies are temporary and are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies stay on your device for a set period or until you delete them, so the site can remember you on your next visit.
  • First-party cookies are set by OasisDeal directly.
  • Third-party cookies are set by our partners whose tools or widgets run on the site.

3. Categories of cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary (essential) — required for the site to function and to remember your basic choices, such as your language preference and your cookie-consent decision. The site cannot work properly without these, so they do not require consent.
  • Analytics / performance — privacy-friendly measurement that helps us understand how visitors use the site (such as which pages are popular) so we can improve it. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you.
  • Partner & affiliate — when you interact with a partner widget (such as a hotel map or price tool) or click through to a partner, that partner may set cookies to run their tools and to attribute the referral to us, which is how we earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
  • Functional — remember choices you make to give you a better experience.

Each of these third parties is responsible for its own cookies, which are governed by that party's own cookie and privacy policy.

6. Your consent

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are always active because the site cannot work without them. We only set analytics and partner cookies in line with your choice. You can change your decision at any time (see below).

You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's cookies and local storage in your browser, which will bring the consent banner back. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings — most browsers let you block all or third-party cookies, delete existing cookies, or warn you before one is set.
