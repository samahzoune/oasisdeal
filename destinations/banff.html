Banff

Turquoise glacier lakes and towering Rocky Mountain peaks.

Cheapest price—live lowest price now

Banff National Park in the Canadian Rockies is a wonderland of turquoise lakes — Louise and Moraine — glaciers and pine forest. Fly into Calgary and drive up. Summer for hiking and canoeing, winter for world-class skiing.

Top things to do

  • See turquoise Lake Louise & Moraine Lake
  • Ride the Banff Gondola
  • Spot wildlife on a scenic drive
  • Ski or snowboard in winter
