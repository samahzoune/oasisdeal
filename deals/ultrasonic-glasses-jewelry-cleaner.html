Frequently asked questions

What can I safely clean in it?

Eyeglasses, most rings and chains, watches (remove leather straps), coins and small metal or plastic items clean up well. Avoid soft or porous gemstones like pearls, opals and emeralds, which can be damaged.

Do I need special cleaning fluid?

Plain water works for light cleaning; a drop of mild dish soap or a dedicated solution boosts results for greasy or heavily soiled items. You don't need expensive chemicals.
