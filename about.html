About OasisDeal

Your savings oasis for travel — compare cheap flights from hundreds of airlines in one search.

OasisDeal exists for one simple reason: finding a cheap flight shouldn't mean opening twenty tabs. Prices for the same trip can vary wildly between airlines and booking sites, and the lowest fare is easy to miss. We bring them all together so you can see the cheapest option at a glance.

What we do

When you search a route, we check hundreds of airlines and online travel agencies at once and line up the results from cheapest to most expensive. You pick the fare you like and book directly with the airline or agency — at the same price they offer, with no markup or booking fee from us.

How we help you save

  • One search, every option. Compare hundreds of airlines and travel sites side by side instead of checking them one by one.
  • Cheapest first. Results are ranked by price, so the lowest fare is right at the top.
  • Flexible dates. Try nearby days to spot the cheapest time to fly your route.

How we make money

OasisDeal is free to use. When you book through a link on our site, we may earn a small commission from the airline or travel partner — at no extra cost to you. You pay exactly what you'd pay booking direct. That commission is what keeps the search free and running.
