About OasisDeal

Your savings oasis for travel — compare cheap flights and hotels in one search, and claim what airlines owe you.

OasisDeal exists for one simple reason: finding the best travel price shouldn't mean opening twenty tabs. Prices for the same flight or hotel vary wildly between sites, and the lowest one is easy to miss. We bring them all together so you can see the cheapest option at a glance.

Who's behind OasisDeal

We're a small, independent team of travelers who got tired of overpaying for flights — and of watching friends and family never claim the compensation airlines owed them. We built OasisDeal for travelers everywhere: people who want to compare honestly, pay a fair price, and have their rights respected. We're not a giant brokerage and we're not anonymous — you can reach a real person here any time.

We follow passenger-rights law closely (including EU EC 261 — worth up to €600) and compare live prices across hundreds of airlines and booking sites, so the information here reflects how flights actually price today, wherever you're flying from — not vague, generic advice.

What we do

When you search, we check hundreds of airlines, travel agencies, and booking sites at once and line up the results from cheapest to most expensive. You pick what you like and book directly with the airline or site — at the same price they offer, with no markup or booking fee from us. And if a flight let you down, our compensation tool helps you claim up to €600 under EU law.

How we help you save

  • One search, every option. Compare hundreds of airlines, agencies, and booking sites side by side.
  • Cheapest first. Results are ranked by price, so the lowest is right at the top.
  • Claim what you're owed. Delayed or cancelled flight? We help you recover compensation.

How we make money

OasisDeal is free to use. When you book or claim through a link on our site, we may earn a small commission from the partner — at no extra cost to you. You pay exactly what you'd pay booking direct. That commission is what keeps the search free and running.

Because commissions are paid by our partners out of their own margin, they never change the price you pay and never influence how we rank or present options. You can read the full detail in our Affiliate Disclosure.

Everything we help you with

OasisDeal started with flights, but today we bring together every part of a trip in one honest place, so you don't have to hop between a dozen sites:

  • Flights. Compare hundreds of airlines and travel agencies in a single search and book directly at the price they offer — no markup, no booking fee from us.
  • Hotels & stays. See the same hotel priced across hundreds of booking sites at once, so you can grab the lowest rate instead of guessing.
  • Airport transfers. Book a fixed-price private ride from the airport to your hotel, with your driver waiting and your flight tracked.
  • Travel eSIMs. Compare data plans across providers so you land already online — no roaming bills and no SIM kiosks.
  • Car rental. Compare rental options for your trip (coming soon).
  • Flight compensation. If your flight was delayed, cancelled or overbooked, our tool checks your eligibility and helps you claim up to €600 under EU rules — money most travellers never realise they're owed.

What makes us different

  • Cheapest first, always. We rank by price for you, not by who pays us the most.
  • Real, live prices. We show prices as they actually are today, pulled from our partners — not vague estimates or stale numbers.
  • No fees, no markup. You book directly with the airline, hotel or provider at their own price.
  • Honest by default. If a deal isn't good, we won't dress it up. Our guides aim to genuinely help you decide, not just push a sale.
  • Your rights matter. We take passenger-rights law seriously and make it easy to claim what airlines owe you.
  • A real team you can reach. We're independent and not anonymous — a real person answers every message.

Our commitment to honest comparison

Comparison sites only work when you can trust them. That's why our rankings are driven by clear, traveller-first criteria, why we clearly label estimated or starting prices, and why we always send you to the provider to confirm the final price and conditions before you pay. We'd rather earn a loyal traveller than a quick, misleading click. When something is uncertain — visa rules, entry requirements, whether a claim will succeed — we say so and point you to official sources rather than pretend to certainty we don't have.

Who OasisDeal is for

We built OasisDeal for travellers everywhere — solo explorers, families, budget backpackers and business travellers alike — who want to compare honestly, pay a fair price, and travel with their rights respected. Wherever you're flying from and whatever your budget, our goal is the same: help you find your savings oasis and keep more money for the trip itself.
