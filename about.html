About OasisDeal

Your savings oasis for travel — compare cheap flights and hotels in one search, and claim what airlines owe you.

OasisDeal exists for one simple reason: finding the best travel price shouldn't mean opening twenty tabs. Prices for the same flight or hotel vary wildly between sites, and the lowest one is easy to miss. We bring them all together so you can see the cheapest option at a glance.

Who's behind OasisDeal

We're a small, independent team based in Casablanca, Morocco — travelers ourselves who got tired of overpaying for flights home and of friends and family never claiming the compensation airlines owed them. We built OasisDeal for the Moroccan community at home and abroad: people who fly between Morocco and Europe, the Gulf and beyond, and want a fair price and their rights respected. We're not a giant brokerage and we're not anonymous — you can reach a real person here any time.

We follow EU passenger-rights law (EC 261) closely and track Moroccan routes daily, so the information on this site reflects how flights and fares from Morocco actually work — not generic global advice.

What we do

When you search, we check hundreds of airlines, travel agencies, and booking sites at once and line up the results from cheapest to most expensive. You pick what you like and book directly with the airline or site — at the same price they offer, with no markup or booking fee from us. And if a flight let you down, our compensation tool helps you claim up to €600 under EU law.

How we help you save

  • One search, every option. Compare hundreds of airlines, agencies, and booking sites side by side.
  • Cheapest first. Results are ranked by price, so the lowest is right at the top.
  • Claim what you're owed. Delayed or cancelled flight? We help you recover compensation.

How we make money

OasisDeal is free to use. When you book or claim through a link on our site, we may earn a small commission from the partner — at no extra cost to you. You pay exactly what you'd pay booking direct. That commission is what keeps the search free and running.
