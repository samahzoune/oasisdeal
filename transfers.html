Good to know

Airport transfer FAQs

Is the transfer price fixed?+

Yes. You see the full price up front and it doesn't change — no meter, no surge pricing and no surprise charges on arrival. What you book is what you pay.

What is meet & greet?+

Your driver waits for you in the arrivals hall holding a sign with your name. If your flight is delayed, the driver tracks it for free and adjusts your pickup, so you never lose your ride.

Where can I book a transfer?+

In more than 150 countries — between airports, hotels, ports, train stations and private addresses. You can search between almost any two locations worldwide.

Can I cancel a transfer?+

Most transfers can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before pickup, with a full refund. The exact terms are shown before you pay.
