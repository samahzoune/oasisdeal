Hotel FAQs

Does OasisDeal charge a booking fee?+

No. We compare prices and send you to the booking site to complete your reservation. You pay the same price you'd pay there — we may earn a small commission from the partner at no extra cost to you.

How do you find cheap hotels?+

Our search checks hundreds of booking sites at the same time for the same hotel and dates, then shows you the lowest price — so you don't have to open a dozen tabs to compare.

When is the best time to book a hotel?+

Prices move with demand. Booking a few weeks ahead and staying flexible on dates usually gives the best rates, and comparing across sites almost always beats booking the first price you see.
