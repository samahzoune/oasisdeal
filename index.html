Flight FAQs

Does OasisDeal charge a booking fee?+

No. We compare prices and route you to the airline or agency — you pay the exact price they offer. We may earn a small commission behind the scenes, never added to your price.

How do you find the cheapest prices?+

Every search scans hundreds of airlines and online travel agencies at once and sorts the results from cheapest to priciest, so the best price for your route floats straight to the top.

When's the best time to book?+

For most routes, a few weeks to two months ahead gives the lowest prices. Staying flexible on dates helps too — try a few nearby days to spot the cheapest one to fly.

Can I book hotels and claim compensation too?+

Yes — the Hotels tab compares stays the same way we compare flights, and the Compensation tab helps you claim up to €600 for a delayed or cancelled flight.
