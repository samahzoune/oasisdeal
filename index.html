Flight FAQs

Does OasisDeal charge a booking fee?

No. We compare prices and send you to the airline or travel agency to book. You pay the same price you'd pay there — we may earn a small commission from the partner at no extra cost to you.

How do you find cheap flights?

Our search checks hundreds of airlines and online travel agencies at the same time and ranks the results by price, so you instantly see the lowest fare for your route and dates.

When is the best time to book?

For most routes, booking a few weeks to a couple of months ahead and staying flexible on dates gives the lowest fares. Try nearby dates in the search to spot the cheapest day to fly.
